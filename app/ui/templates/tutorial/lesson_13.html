13. Ask again on a timer

Lesson 12 asks once, when the box reaches the screen. This box asks on its own, every two seconds, and shows the time on the server.

Try it

Wait. After two seconds the sentence in the box becomes the clock, then it updates again two seconds later.

The time appears here, then updates every two seconds.

What changed

The new trigger is every 2s. HTMX waits two seconds, sends the request, waits two seconds, and sends it again. There is no button. The box is its own target, and innerHTML replaces the sentence inside it. The attributes stay on the box, so the timer stays too. The fragment is the clock in UTC, so a later response shows a later second. The address does not change.

The markup and the fragment

<div
  id="server-clock"
  hx-get="/ui/tutorial/13/clock"
  hx-target="#server-clock"
  hx-swap="innerHTML"
  hx-trigger="every 2s"
>...</div>

GET /ui/tutorial/13/clock is lesson_clock in app/ui/tutorial_routes.py. It returns tutorial/clock.html: the hour, minute, and second in UTC. It does not sleep. This URL is only that clock. It is not the books table, and it does not change the address.

Request, response, target, swap

  • Request: GET /ui/tutorial/13/clock every two seconds.
  • Response: the current time.
  • Target: #server-clock, the box itself.
  • Swap: innerHTML.

Experiment

In the inspector, set hx-trigger="click" and wait. The clock stops where it is. A click fetches once. Set every 2s again and the updates resume.
